Architectural Heritage & Luxury

A Historic 1916 Church Reborn as Sister Bay's Premier Estate

Welcome to Maple Manor, one of Door County's most extraordinary vacation properties. Originally built in 1916 as the Sister Bay Baptist Church (on land donated in 1882 by pioneer J.J. Wiltse), this architectural landmark has been meticulously restored and masterfully transformed into a 7,000 sq. ft. luxury sanctuary that accommodates up to 22 guests in beds across 8 spacious bedrooms.

Step inside the grand Cathedral Great Room, where 25-foot soaring timber-beamed ceilings, illuminated library shelving, an acoustic piano, and a large central gas fireplace create an inspiring gathering space. Just steps away, our gourmet eat-in kitchen features cherry wood cabinetry, expansive granite prep islands, dual refrigerators, and seating for 10, connecting directly to the formal dining room (seating 10) so your entire group of 20+ can dine together comfortably.

Beyond the main level, discover the 2,000 sq. ft. exposed lower-level recreation floor with 9-foot ceilings, a full-size billiards table, 12-ft custom shuffleboard table, 75” 4K Smart TV lounge, gaming tables, and a second laundry suite. Outside, enjoy a lush half-acre county backyard with a large entertaining deck, BBQ grill, and massive campfire pit.

Best of all, Maple Manor is located right inside the village limits: a scenic 1/4-mile walk (5 minutes) to the Sister Bay Beach, Marina, Waterfront Park, and legendary dining spots like Al Johnson's, Wild Tomato, Chop, Lure, Husby's, and the Boathouse.
